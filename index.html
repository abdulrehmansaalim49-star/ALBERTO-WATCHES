    HELPFUL HOROLOGY ANSWERS

    Frequently asked questions

    01 How do I arrange an official watch repair?

    Contact or visit your nearest Alberto store in Karachi, Lahore, or Islamabad with the watch. Our master horologists provide a free diagnostic assessment within 24 hours.

    02 How long does full movement servicing take?

    Simple battery and gasket seal work is completed within 45 minutes. Complete mechanical overhauls generally take 3 to 7 working days depending on parts.

    03 Can Alberto provide official insurance appraisals?

    Yes. Bring the timepiece with its original box or papers. We issue formal valuation certificates containing serial verification, gold purity, and replacement value.

    04 Are all Alberto timepieces priced in Pakistani Rupees?

    Yes. All prices in our official store and online catalogue are transparently displayed in Pakistani Rupees (PKR), inclusive of all certified import inspections.

    05 How should I care for my automatic timepiece?

    Keep your watch away from intense magnetic fields (speakers, MRI). Ensure screw-down crowns are securely closed before water contact, and service every 4 to 5 years.

    06 What support is provided for Smart Watches?

    Our smart series includes full 2-year hardware replacement, pairing support for iOS and Android, and replacement charging cables at all boutiques.
